Owntend Account & Data Deletion

Sign in with the Google account linked to Owntend to permanently delete its cloud account and synchronized data.

What will be deleted?

  • Your Owntend authentication account and its link to your Google identity.
  • Your synchronized homes, rooms, assets, maintenance records, and related cloud data.
  • Your photos and other media stored in Owntend private cloud storage.

What is outside this request?

Backups you exported outside Owntend are not controlled by this request. Data independently processed by Google or other service providers is governed by their policies. This browser flow cannot remove Owntend authorization from your Google Account; you may revoke it separately in your Google Account settings.

Other deletion method

You can also delete your account in Owntend under Settings → Account → Delete Account.
